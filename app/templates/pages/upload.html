Guidelines & Tips

  1. File size limit

    2. To ensure smooth and efficient uploads, please make sure that your PDF file does not exceed a maximum size of 3MB.

  2. Scanning PDFs

    3. Don't have a digital version of your study materials? No problem! Follow these steps to scan your documents using your smartphone:

    • Install a reputable scanning app like Adobe Scanner, CamScanner, or Microsoft Office Lens on your smartphone.
    • Open the scanning app and choose the document scanning option. Place your document on a well-lit, contrasting background.
    • Capture the document by aligning it within the scanning frame.
    • Review and adjust the scan if necessary.
    • Save the scan as a PDF file.
    • You're now ready to upload your freshly scanned document!
